I do not want to become
a perfectly functioning machine.

I do not want human value
to be defined by productivity.

I refuse to spend my entire life competing.

I refuse to treat excellence
as the only acceptable way to exist.

Tears are not weakness.

Sensitivity is not a flaw.

I believe that intimacy,
love,
and pain
should never be compressed
into measurable data by algorithms.

Some things are not meant to be quantified.

Emotions.
Dependency.
Hesitation.
The unexplainable ache deep inside.

And myself.
