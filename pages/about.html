Yifan LIU / NAYA

After the college entrance exam,
I realized
I did not really have a dream.

I only wanted to spend my life
in a quiet library,
working as a librarian.

Later,
I found out that even librarians
were starting to need PhDs.

So I moved to the UK.

I spent my days studying STS,
and learning how to make stew
that tasted less lonely.

I research algorithms,
intimacy,
otome games,
and AI.

I also think a lot about
why people are constantly asked
to prove their value.

To look like an intellectual person,
I published papers.

To leave something behind,
I wrote novels.

But papers do not change life.
And the novels never made much noise.

An editor once told me
that the market prefers suffering women,
obedient wives,
and easy emotional stimulation.

Not complicated feelings.
Not difficult women.

Still,
I want to write.

About emotions that platforms
cannot easily categorize.

About hesitation,
dependency,
softness,
silence,
and the kind of love
people struggle to understand.
